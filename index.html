Dear Reighn,

Happy 19th birthday! Somehow this year already has enough stories for an entire season of a show.

You met people, caught feelings, got hurt, took chances, and even went all the way to Malabon for someone. But none of those experiences determine your worth. They are just chapters in a much bigger story, and you still get to decide what happens next.

As your friend Astro, I hope 19 gives you better people, genuine happiness, fewer unnecessary plot twists, and no more going out of your way for someone who doesn't know your value.

I hope this birthday reminds you that even when some chapters don't end the way you wanted, the story itself is still worth continuing.

Happy birthday, Reighn Yzabelle Pagdanganan.
